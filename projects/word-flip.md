# Juggle Learning Word Flip

## Interactive early-literacy learning experience

**Status:** Active product prototype  
**Focus:** phonics, early literacy, instructional product design, educational technology

### The problem

Many digital phonics activities isolate one task at a time. A child may identify a sound, click a word, or complete a matching activity without connecting the sound pattern to reading, writing, and meaningful sentence use.

Word Flip was designed to connect those pieces in one learner experience.

### The experience

Children move through a sequence that can include:

- Hearing and identifying sounds
- Reading a target word
- Building the word with letter tiles
- Practicing written production
- Reconstructing a related sentence
- Connecting the sentence to a visual scene
- Receiving audio support as needed

The experience includes phonics patterns across early decoding skills while keeping the interaction visually focused.

### Instructional design decisions

- Sound practice is connected to real word reading
- Word building comes before sentence application
- Narration and highlighting support attention to print
- Visuals support meaning without becoming the task
- Optional support is available when a learner needs more practice with a sound
- The experience is designed to complement hands-on literacy products rather than simply reproduce a worksheet on screen

### Technology

The current prototype uses React, Vite, TypeScript, Express, and locally managed media assets. The production version is designed to run without exposing credentials or requiring client-side secrets.

### What this project demonstrates

- Translating literacy expertise into product behavior
- Designing digital practice around a clear instructional sequence
- Combining audio, print, interaction, and visual context
- Product iteration based on learner experience
- Building technology around instructional intent rather than adding technology for its own sake
