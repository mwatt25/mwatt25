# Keep the Human

## Interactive AI literacy and decision-making simulation

**Status:** Active prototype  
**Focus:** AI literacy, educator decision-making, professional learning, implementation

### The problem

AI training can become a list of tools and rules without helping educators practice the judgment required in real situations.

Keep the Human was designed around a different question:

**What happens when an educator has to make the decision, with realistic tradeoffs, before being told the answer?**

### The experience

Participants move through school-based scenarios and make decisions about how AI should or should not be used. Choices are followed by visible decision impact across four lenses:

- **People:** Who is helped, burdened, included, or left out?
- **Privacy:** What information is being used and what should remain protected?
- **Quality:** Does the decision improve the work without lowering professional judgment or instructional quality?
- **Capacity:** What workload is reduced, shifted, or created?

The experience is designed to make responsible AI use feel concrete rather than theoretical.

### Design choices

- First-person scenario format rather than slide-based instruction
- Immediate rationale after a choice
- Consequences tied to the user's actual decisions
- Evidence checks that show which considerations were used or missed
- Reflection and classroom commitment at the end of the experience
- Multiple school contexts rather than one generic AI example
- Captions and navigation controls built into the experience

### Technology

The current prototype uses React, Framer Motion, esbuild, and locally managed media assets.

### What this project demonstrates

- Translating an abstract AI-readiness topic into applied learning
- Scenario and branching-decision design
- Connecting UX choices to instructional goals
- Designing AI guidance around human judgment rather than tool novelty
- Iterating from user experience feedback

### What is intentionally not public

The production repository, media library, full scenario logic, scoring configuration, and development history are private. This case study is the public portfolio view.
