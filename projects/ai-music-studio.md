# AI Music Studio

## AI-supported creative learning experience

**Status:** Portfolio project  
**Focus:** AI learning design, creative workflow, multimodal instruction

### The concept

AI Music Studio was designed as a structured creative learning experience rather than a collection of disconnected AI tools.

Participants move through a studio-inspired sequence that mirrors a real creative process:

1. Develop the concept
2. Work through the writing process
3. Build the audio or recording direction
4. Create the visual direction
5. Prepare the release
6. Present the final work

### Why the structure matters

Creative AI tools can produce impressive outputs quickly, but learners still need a process for making intentional choices.

The studio structure gives each tool a job inside a larger workflow. The learner remains responsible for the concept, direction, revision, and final decision-making.

### Instructional approach

- Clear stages rather than an open-ended tool list
- Creative decisions before generation
- Revision built into the process
- Multiple media types connected to one final product
- Emphasis on authorship and intentional use of AI

### What this project demonstrates

- Designing instruction around a multi-step AI workflow
- Scaffolding creative work without over-directing the learner
- Connecting text, audio, visual, and presentation tasks
- Teaching process rather than only software features
- Translating emerging technology into a learnable experience

### Public portfolio scope

This case study summarizes the learning design. Detailed lesson content and instructional materials are treated separately from the public portfolio.
